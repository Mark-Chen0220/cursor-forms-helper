Cursor Forms Helper

Cursor Forms Helper is a personal tool that lets Mark Chen create, edit, publish, and read Google Forms from the Cursor editor.

It uses the official Google Forms API with OAuth. After Mark signs in with his Google Account, the helper can:

  • Create a Google Form and set its title and description
  • Add or update questions (text, multiple choice, dropdown, date, and similar types)
  • Publish or unpublish a form and open or close responses
  • Read form structure and submitted responses

This is not a public product, a Google product, or a form-filling service for other people. Only Mark’s Google Account is intended to authorize it.
