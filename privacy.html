Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy describes how Cursor Forms Helper (“the app”) accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data. The app is a personal integration used by Mark Chen to manage his own Google Forms from Cursor.

Google user data the app accesses

With your Google Account permission, the app can:

  • Create and edit Google Forms you can already access, including titles, descriptions, questions, quiz settings, and publish settings (forms.body).
  • Read responses submitted to those forms (forms.responses.readonly).

The app does not request Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or full Drive access. It does not submit form answers as a respondent.

How the app uses that data

Google Forms data is used only to carry out actions you ask for in Cursor, such as creating a form, adding questions, publishing a form, or summarizing responses. It is not used for advertising, analytics products, credit decisions, or training public AI models.

How the app stores that data

Form content and responses stay in your Google Account. The app does not keep a separate forms database.

OAuth client ID, client secret, and refresh token are stored as environment variables on Mark’s computer so Cursor can call the Google Forms API. Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed locally.

How the app shares that data

API requests are sent to Google (forms.googleapis.com). The app does not sell Google user data. It does not share Google user data with other people, advertisers, or unrelated services.

A local Model Context Protocol process on Mark’s computer (mcp-google-forms) performs the API calls. Telemetry for that process is disabled. Cursor processes your prompts on your machine in order to decide which API calls to make.

Limited use

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Forms and responses remain in Google Forms until you delete them in Google. You can revoke the app’s access at any time in your Google Account under Manage your Google Account → Security → Third-party access. After you revoke access, delete the local environment variables GOOGLE_FORMS_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_FORMS_CLIENT_SECRET, and GOOGLE_FORMS_REFRESH_TOKEN.
